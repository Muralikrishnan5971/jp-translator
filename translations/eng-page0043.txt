Chapter 8: Daily Life During Pregnancy (Continued)
1. Driving during pregnancy
- The author says that pregnant women can generally continue driving.
- Some women may be told that driving is dangerous during pregnancy, but the author questions this belief.
- Driving a car does not automatically cause problems during pregnancy.
- However, pregnant women should be especially careful on the road.
- As pregnancy progresses, the growing abdomen may make driving uncomfortable.
- The author advises avoiding unnecessary risks.
2. Exercise and physical activity during pregnancy
- The author explains that pregnancy naturally causes weight gain.
- As the baby grows, the mother's body becomes heavier.
- Pregnant women may feel that moving around is more difficult.
- However, the author warns against becoming completely inactive.
- Physical activity is still important during pregnancy.
- The author mentions that bicycle riding may become more dangerous during the later stages because of the risk of falling.
3. Clothing during pregnancy
- Pregnant women should wear comfortable clothing.
- Clothes should not put excessive pressure on the abdomen.
- The author discusses maternity dresses designed to accommodate a growing belly.
- Tight clothing may become uncomfortable as pregnancy progresses.
- Clothing should allow women to move freely.
4. Maternity belts and abdominal support
- The author discusses maternity belts, which are worn around the abdomen.
- Some pregnant women use these belts to support their growing belly.
- The author explains that maternity belts are not always medically necessary.
- Wearing a belt does not directly make the baby smaller or prevent premature delivery.
- However, some women may feel more comfortable when wearing one.
- The author suggests that personal comfort should be considered.
5. Sexual intercourse during pregnancy
- The author discusses whether sexual intercourse is safe during pregnancy.
- According to the text, sexual activity does not necessarily need to stop during a normal pregnancy.
- However, the author warns that sexual intercourse may sometimes be associated with complications.
- The book mentions that intercourse during the later stages of pregnancy was sometimes believed to increase the risk of premature delivery.
- The author emphasizes that women should pay attention to their physical condition.

Chapter 9: When the Nipples Are Retracted (Inverted Nipples)
6. What are inverted nipples?
- Some women have nipples that point inward instead of outward.
- This condition is called inverted nipples.
- Women with inverted nipples may worry about breastfeeding after childbirth.
- The author explains that inverted nipples do not always prevent breastfeeding.
- Even when the nipple is inverted, the baby may still be able to breastfeed.
7. Breastfeeding with inverted nipples
- The author says that breastfeeding may still be possible even when the nipples are inverted.
- Babies do not suck only the nipple; they also take part of the areola into their mouths.
- Therefore, the shape of the nipple alone does not determine whether breastfeeding is possible.
- The author encourages mothers not to become overly worried.
8. Preparing the nipples before childbirth
- The author describes a method intended to make inverted nipples protrude.
- The suggested technique involves gently pressing around the nipple with the fingers.
- The author recommends using the thumb and index finger to apply pressure around the nipple.
- The nipple is then gently pulled outward.
- According to the book, this can be repeated several times each day.
9. When to begin nipple preparation
- The author suggests beginning nipple exercises around the 18th week of pregnancy.
- The text recommends repeating the exercise several times a day.
- It suggests continuing the practice until delivery.
- The author believes that regular exercises may help prepare the nipples for breastfeeding.
10. Babies and breastfeeding
- The author explains that newborn babies naturally have a sucking reflex.
- Babies can often breastfeed successfully even when the mother's nipples are not prominent.
- The author emphasizes that breastfeeding is a natural process.
- Mothers should not automatically assume that inverted nipples will prevent them from feeding their babies.
11. Breastfeeding devices
- The author mentions devices designed to pull inverted nipples outward.
- These include plastic devices shaped like a doughnut that press around the nipple.
- Other devices use suction to pull the nipple outward.
- The author questions whether these products are truly effective.
- The text suggests that some commercially available breastfeeding devices may not provide the benefits advertised.

Main message of this page
The first part explains that pregnant women can generally continue normal activities, including driving, exercise, and sexual activity, while taking suitable precautions.

The second part explains inverted nipples, their possible effects on breastfeeding, and traditional methods suggested for preparing the nipples before childbirth.

Important medical context: Routine nipple pulling, stretching, or manipulation during pregnancy is not recommended as a proven way to prevent breastfeeding difficulties. Many women with inverted nipples can breastfeed successfully with proper positioning and support from a lactation specialist. Sexual intercourse is generally safe during an uncomplicated pregnancy, but certain conditions, such as placenta previa, ruptured membranes, or a risk of preterm labor, may require avoiding it.
