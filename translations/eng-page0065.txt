Chapter 26: Baby's Clothes and Diapers (赤ちゃんの衣類とおむつ)
1. Choosing clothes for a newborn
1. Babies should wear warm clothes in winter and light, comfortable clothes in summer.
2. Clothes should allow the baby's skin to breathe and sweat to evaporate.
3. Babies should be able to move their hands and legs freely.
4. Choose clothes that do not restrict movement.
5. The most important thing is to maintain a comfortable body temperature.
6. Avoid dressing babies in too many layers.
2. Avoid buying too many baby clothes
1. Department stores sell many different types of baby clothes.
2. Some clothes are expensive and decorated with unnecessary features.
3. Parents should avoid buying clothes simply because they look attractive.
4. Clothes with wide openings are easier to put on and remove.
5. Clothes that are too tight or too loose can be uncomfortable.
6. Babies grow quickly, so clothes become too small within a short time.
7. It is better to buy simple, practical clothes that can be easily replaced.
3. Choosing suitable materials
1. The book says cotton is generally a good material for baby clothes.
2. Cotton is soft and comfortable against the skin.
3. The author also mentions that synthetic materials are not necessarily bad.
4. The important thing is how the material feels against the baby's skin.
5. Choose clothes that are easy to wash and dry.
6. Parents do not need to purchase expensive branded clothes.
4. Baby diapers
1. Diapers are one of the most important items needed for a newborn.
2. The book discusses both cloth diapers and disposable diapers.
3. Cloth diapers can be washed and reused.
4. Parents should prepare enough diapers for frequent changes.
5. The baby's skin should be kept clean and dry.
6. Diapers should be changed whenever they become wet or dirty.

The author emphasizes that newborn babies need simple, comfortable, practical clothes rather than expensive or fashionable clothing.

The main recommendations are:
1. Choose clothing suitable for the weather.
2. Allow the baby to move freely.
3. Prefer soft, comfortable fabrics.
4. Avoid buying too many clothes because babies grow quickly.
5. Prepare essential clothing, diapers, and bedding before delivery.
6. Focus on the baby's comfort rather than appearance.
Modern safety note: The checklist reflects older Japanese childcare practices. Electric heating pads, hot-water bottles, loose blankets, and soft bedding should not be placed in a newborn's sleep space because of overheating, burns, or suffocation risks. Current guidance recommends a firm, flat sleep surface with a fitted sheet and no loose bedding.
