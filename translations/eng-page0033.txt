“Can You Become a Mother?” 
It discusses whether a woman should have a child, the responsibilities of parenthood, and the challenges of raising a child when there are health or other difficulties.

1. Should you become a mother?
After marriage, a woman should consider becoming a mother rather than delaying it indefinitely because of work or financial reasons.

The author argues that having children by the age of 30 may be preferable because fertility and physical stamina can decline with age.

Raising a child requires considerable physical effort.

The author mentions that the risk of Down syndrome increases with maternal age.

Not feeling confident about raising a child is not, by itself, a good reason to avoid having one. Parenting is something people learn through experience.

2. You do not need to know everything about parenting
Nobody is born knowing how to raise a child.

Raising a child is not like taking an examination where you must know every answer.

You only need to learn what your baby needs at each stage of development.

For example, learn what a one-month-old baby needs first. When the baby reaches four months, learn about that stage.

You do not need to worry about every possible childhood illness before your baby is even born.

3. You do not have to be a perfect person to become a parent
The author disagrees with the idea that someone must become a perfect human being before raising a child.
Human beings are never completely perfect.
Parenthood can help people grow and mature.
Having a child can be an opportunity to become a better person.
From a child's perspective, a parent who is overly confident is not necessarily a good parent.
A god parent is someone who explores life alongside the child and grows together with them.

4. What if you have a medical condition?
Some people hesitate to become parents because they have an illness.

The author says medical science is advancing, so having a health condition does not automatically mean someone cannot become a parent.

People with chronic kidney disease may still be able to give birth, depending on their condition and medical care.

People with diabetes may also be able to have children if their blood sugar is properly controlled.

Even if a disease runs in the family, people should not immediately give up on having children.

If someone can live a normal life without seriously harming themselves or others, the author encourages them to consider choosing life rather than automatically assuming the worst.

5. What if you cannot conceive a child?
Wanting to become a mother does not guarantee that pregnancy will happen.

The author says infertility is generally considered when a couple has been unable to conceive after two years of marriage.

If a couple wants a child and pregnancy has not occurred after one year, they can consider consulting a doctor.

Infertility should not automatically be blamed on the woman.

Both partners should undergo medical evaluation because the cause may be related to either partner.

The author points out that male infertility is not uncommon.

Main message of the page
The central idea is that you do not need to be perfect, completely confident, or free from every health problem to consider parenthood. Parenting is a process of learning and growing together with a child.
