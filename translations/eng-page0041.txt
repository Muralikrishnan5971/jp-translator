Chapter 8: Daily Life During Pregnancy
4. Should pregnant women change their daily routine?
- The author says that pregnancy does not require a sudden or complete change in daily life.
- Most women can continue their normal activities.
- However, they should avoid unnecessary physical strain.
- The author emphasizes that women should take reasonable care of their health.
- Pregnancy should not be treated as an illness, but proper precautions are important.
5. Food and nutrition during pregnancy
- The author discusses how eating habits have changed over time.
- In the past, many people in Japan did not have enough nutritious food.
- Pregnant women were often advised to eat more.
- However, the author says that modern diets may already provide sufficient calories.
- Therefore, simply eating more food is not always necessary.
- The quality of food is more important than the quantity.
6. How many calories are needed?
- The book mentions that Japanese women generally consume around 1,800 calories per day.
- It states that pregnant women may need approximately 150 additional calories.
- The author suggests that eating more than necessary may lead to excessive weight gain.
- Pregnant women should focus on getting enough nutrients rather than simply increasing calories.
7. Choosing nutritious foods
- The author recommends eating a balanced diet containing different types of food.
- Protein-rich foods are particularly important.
- Examples mentioned include:
  - Milk.
  - Fish.
  - Meat.
  - Cheese.
  - Eggs.
- These foods provide nutrients needed for the mother's health and the baby's development.
- The author suggests that adding a small amount of nutritious food may be better than eating large quantities of ordinary food.
8. Eating more does not always mean better nutrition
- The author explains that overeating is unnecessary during pregnancy.
- Some women may think they must eat enough for two people.
- However, the author argues that the baby does not require the same amount of food as an adult.
- Pregnant women should concentrate on eating nutritious meals.
- Excessive calorie intake may cause unnecessary weight gain.
9. Weight gain during pregnancy
- The author says that body weight normally increases during pregnancy.
- Weight gain is necessary because the baby, placenta, and other pregnancy-related tissues are developing.
- However, excessive weight gain may cause problems.
- The author recommends monitoring body weight regularly.
- A healthy diet and appropriate physical activity can help manage weight gain.
10. Changes in appetite
- Pregnancy can affect appetite and eating habits.
- Some women may experience nausea or difficulty eating.
- Others may feel hungrier than usual.
- The author says that eating patterns should be adjusted according to the woman's condition.
- However, nutritious foods should remain an important part of the diet.

- The author discusses how nutritional requirements change as pregnancy progresses.

Main message of this page
The author explains that pregnant women should choose a convenient hospital, attend regular checkups, and maintain proper health records. During pregnancy, women can generally continue their normal daily activities while paying attention to nutrition, weight gain, and overall health.

Important medical context: The calorie recommendations in this older Japanese book are not current universal guidelines. Modern recommendations vary by pre-pregnancy weight, activity level, and trimester. Many guidelines suggest approximately 340 additional calories per day in the second trimester and 450 in the third trimester for women starting pregnancy at a healthy weight.
