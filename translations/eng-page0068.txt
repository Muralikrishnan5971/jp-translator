New Section: From Birth to One Week Old
Japanese title: 誕生から1週まで (Tanjō kara Isshū made)
1. Meaning of the title
1. 誕生 (Tanjō) means Birth.
2. から (Kara) means From.
3. 1週 (Isshū) means One week.
4. まで (Made) means Until.
Complete translation: "From Birth to the End of the First Week."
2. What is on this page?
1. This is the opening page of a new section in the book.
2. The previous section discussed preparations before the baby is born.
3. The new section focuses on the baby's first week after birth.
4. The page contains a photograph of a sleeping newborn baby.
5. There is no additional main text to translate on this page.
3. Main message
The book is moving from preparing for childbirth to caring for a newborn during the first seven days of life.

The next pages will contain the actual explanations for this new section.
