Main message of this page
The author explains that pregnant women should eat nutritious food, avoid excessive vitamin intake, remain physically active within comfortable limits, and take sensible precautions while driving, travelling, and bathing. Pregnancy usually does not require abandoning normal daily activities.

Important medical context: Modern guidance recommends 400 micrograms of folic acid daily before conception and during early pregnancy for most women, with higher doses for certain medical indications. Excessive preformed vitamin A can cause birth defects. Current guidance also supports properly positioned three-point seat belts throughout pregnancy. Routine avoidance of peanuts during pregnancy is not recommended solely to prevent allergies in children.
