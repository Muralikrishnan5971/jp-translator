Chapter 18: When Should You Be Admitted to the Hospital?
Japanese title: いつ入院すればいいか
10. Deciding when to go to the hospital
- Around 36 weeks of pregnancy (approximately 10 months in the Japanese pregnancy calendar), pregnant women may begin visiting their doctor every week.
- Women often ask their doctors when they should be admitted to the hospital.
- However, doctors cannot always predict exactly when labor will begin.
- Even if a doctor gives an estimated date, childbirth may happen earlier or later.
- Pregnant women should understand the signs of labor and know when to contact the hospital.
11. The beginning of labor
- The author explains that labor can begin before a woman realizes what is happening.
- Early labor contractions may feel similar to the urge to pass stool.
- Some women mistake these contractions for ordinary abdominal discomfort.
- Because of this, they may not immediately recognize that labor has started.
12. Recognizing labor contractions
- Labor contractions occur when the uterus tightens.
- At first, the contractions may be mild.
- They can feel like abdominal cramps or pressure.
- The author explains that some women may mistake them for constipation or stomach pain.
- As labor progresses, the contractions generally become stronger and more regular.
13. What happens when the cervix opens?
- During labor, the cervix gradually opens to allow the baby to pass through.
- The author explains that a woman may not always recognize the early stages of cervical opening.
- Some women may continue their normal activities without realizing labor has started.
- The author emphasizes the importance of recognizing changes in the body.
14. Waiting at home during early labor
- Some women may stay at home during the early stages of labor.
- The author mentions that women may wait until contractions become more noticeable.
