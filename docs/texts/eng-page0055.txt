Chapter 17: Returning Home for Childbirth (里帰り分娩)
7. What is Satogaeri Bunben?
- In Japan, some pregnant women return to their parents' home to give birth.
- This custom is called Satogaeri Bunben (里帰り分娩).
- It allows the mother to receive support from her parents and relatives.
- The author explains that this practice is common in Japan.
- Many women feel more comfortable giving birth near their families.
8. Why women return to their parents' home
- Some women live far away from their hometowns after marriage.
- They may move to large cities for work or family reasons.
- During pregnancy, they may decide to return to their parents' home.
- This allows them to receive help during childbirth and recovery.
- The author explains that family support is one of the main reasons for this practice.
9. Planning childbirth away from home
- The author recommends planning the delivery location early.
- Pregnant women should arrange medical care before traveling.
- They should identify a hospital or maternity clinic near their parents' home.
- The author explains that waiting until the last moment can create difficulties.
- Proper planning can make childbirth safer and less stressful.
10. Informing the maternity hospital
- Women should tell their doctors if they plan to give birth in another location.
- Medical records may need to be transferred to the new hospital.
- The author recommends contacting the hospital before arriving.
- This helps the medical team understand the woman's pregnancy history.
- The author emphasizes that proper communication between doctors is important.
11. Difficulties during early pregnancy
- The author explains that early pregnancy can involve nausea and other discomforts.
- Some women may experience morning sickness.
- These symptoms can make travel and daily activities difficult.
- Family support may help women manage these problems.
- However, the author says that pregnancy is not necessarily a reason to stop all normal activities.
12. Preparing for the baby's arrival
- The author discusses how families prepare for childbirth.
- Parents may arrange accommodation, medical care, and other necessities.
- Some women prefer to stay with their parents during the final months of pregnancy.
- The author explains that this arrangement can reduce some of the mother's responsibilities.
- However, childbirth preparations should be shared among family members.
13. The husband's role during pregnancy
- The author discusses the responsibilities of husbands during pregnancy.
- Even when the woman returns to her parents' home, her husband still has responsibilities.
- The author suggests that husbands should participate in preparations for childbirth.
- They should support their wives emotionally and practically.
- The author emphasizes that pregnancy and childbirth concern both parents.
14. Sharing household responsibilities
- The author explains that household work should not be considered only the woman's responsibility.
- Husbands can help with cleaning, cooking, and other daily tasks.
- This becomes especially important during pregnancy and after childbirth.
- The author suggests that sharing responsibilities can reduce stress.

Chapter 17: Returning to one's parents' home for childbirth is a Japanese tradition that provides family support. However, the author emphasizes that husbands should also take responsibility for childbirth preparations and raising their children.
