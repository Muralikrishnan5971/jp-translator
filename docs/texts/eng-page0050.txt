- Women with chronic diseases should receive careful medical supervision.

- The author recommends a detailed medical examination before marriage or pregnancy.

- Doctors can also identify possible risks to the baby.
- Planning ahead makes it easier to manage pregnancy safely.

- Women with diabetes or kidney disease should not stop treatment simply because they become pregnant.

Main message of this page
The author explains that women with existing medical conditions, particularly kidney disease and diabetes, can often become pregnant and give birth safely with proper medical supervision.

The most important recommendations are to plan pregnancy carefully, consult doctors before conception, control existing illnesses, and attend regular prenatal examinations.

Important medical context: Modern care recommends preconception assessment for women with diabetes or kidney disease. Diabetes treatment during pregnancy is individualized, and some medications may need to be changed before conception. Blood sugar targets and fetal monitoring schedules should be determined by the treating medical team.
